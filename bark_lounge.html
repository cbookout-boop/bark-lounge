About Services Before Your Visit

A cozy place for your best friend

Hi, I'm Cody, the owner of The Bark Lounge & Co. in Dallas, Texas. I know your dog is part of your family, and I want them to feel comfortable and cared for while you're away.

I work from home full time, so dog care is part of my everyday routine. From overnight stays to a walk around the neighborhood, I take time to get to know each dog's personality and needs.

Care that fits your dog's day

Overnight Boarding

A welcoming home setting while you're out of town, with attention to your dog's feeding schedule, rest, and daily routine.

Dog Sitting

Personal attention and companionship to help your dog feel at ease while you're away.

Drop-In Visits

A check-in for meals, fresh water, potty breaks, and a little company during the day.

Dog Walking

A chance to stretch their legs, explore, and enjoy a walk at a pace that works for them.

Let's get to know your pup

Before a stay, we'll talk about your dog's routine and arrange a meet and greet to see whether we're a good fit. Please let me know how your dog gets along with other dogs.

What to share before a visit

  • Your dog's feeding schedule and usual food.
  • Care instructions and any special needs.
  • Your contact information and an emergency contact.
  • Your dog's temperament and comfort around other dogs.
  • A favorite toy or comfort item for an overnight stay.

Planning a stay?

When you reach out, include your dates, the service you need, and a little about your dog. We'll discuss availability and what will help your pup settle in.
